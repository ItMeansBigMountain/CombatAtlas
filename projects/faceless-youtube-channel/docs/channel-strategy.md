# Channel Strategy

## Working Project Name

Faceless YouTube Channel

## Channel Model Options

Choose one primary lane first:

- **Story/documentary:** internet history, business stories, athlete stories, crime/legal explainers.
- **Money/career:** cloud engineering, tech jobs, no-college career path, AI tools for workers.
- **Self-improvement for first-gen men:** discipline, dopamine, faith/culture tension, fatherlessness, remote work.
- **AI/tools tutorials:** practical automation, workflows, app builds, side hustles.
- **News analysis:** stock/news sentiment, market stories, business breakdowns.

## Recommended Starting Lane

**Faceless documentary + practical self-improvement for under-guided first-gen men entering tech.**

Why:

- Matches the user’s real story and voice.
- Can be faceless with stock footage, b-roll, captions, graphs, AI visuals, and narration.
- Differentiates from generic AI-generated channels.
- Supports future offers, community, coaching, or digital products.

## Audience Promise

"No-fluff breakdowns for under-guided men trying to escape chaos, build discipline, and use tech/AI to change their life."

## Content Pillars

1. **Escape Path:** tech careers, cloud, remote work, no-college routes.
2. **Discipline Systems:** dopamine, food, weed, focus, fitness, routine.
3. **Identity Conflict:** immigrant/Muslim-American pressure, fatherlessness, masculinity, belonging.
4. **AI Leverage:** tools, workflows, faceless content, automation, side projects.
5. **Case Studies:** stories of people, companies, athletes, creators, and trends.

## Voice

Direct, cinematic, practical, emotionally honest. No guru tone. No fake luxury bait.

## Initial Format

- Length: 6–10 minutes.
- Style: strong hook, cinematic narration, fast visual rhythm, captions, concrete lesson.
- Upload cadence: 1 polished video/week for the first 8 weeks.
