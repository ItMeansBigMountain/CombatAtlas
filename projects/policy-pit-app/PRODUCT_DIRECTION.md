# Policy Pit Direction

Policy Pit should compare two opposing politicians and their policies through bite-sized, swipeable, crowd-sourced policy preference data.

## Core UX

- Pick two politicians.
- Pick topics you care about.
- See plain-English policy cards.
- Swipe left if blue-aligned, right if red-aligned, or neutral/skip.
- Show crowd popularity by topic, demographic segment, and politician stance.

## Product promise

Make political policy understandable without long speeches, cable-news framing, or party-first confusion.
