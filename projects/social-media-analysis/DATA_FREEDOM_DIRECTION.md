# Data Freedom Direction

Goal: free people’s minds with data by showing insights they generate through internet use but rarely get to see.

## Product direction

`social-media-analysis`, `tweetBetweenTheLines`, and `twitter-therapy-app` merge into a single Personal Presence Intelligence app.

The user signs in, connects supported social media accounts, or uploads official user-data exports requested from the platform. The app then comprehends their posts/messages/activity to explain topics, mood, sentiment, communication style, mental-health-adjacent patterns, and other useful insights.

## Product ideas

- Personal internet-use data dashboard.
- Social media OAuth/import connectors.
- User-uploaded data archive parsers for ZIP/JSON/CSV/HTML exports.
- Topic, emotion, attention, and time-pattern insights.
- Mood and sentiment timelines.
- Communication style analysis.
- “What platforms know about you” explainers.
- Posts worth reviewing before job/client/public exposure.
- Actionable privacy and behavior-change recommendations.

## Safety boundary

The app can surface patterns like stress markers, rumination, negativity, or volatility. It must not diagnose mental illness. It should use gentle language and route self-harm-risk language toward appropriate support resources.
