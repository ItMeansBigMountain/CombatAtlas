# Robinhood Agentic Trading Rules

- Account sandbox: $200 starting capital.
- Default risk per trade: 1% of account value (~$2 at $200).
- Max aggregate open risk: 3% of account value (~$6 at $200).
- Default instrument: liquid equities only.
- No options unless explicitly requested.
- Minimum R:R: 1.5:1; prefer 2:1+.
- Every setup needs entry, stop, target, size, max loss, potential reward, and invalidation.
- Preview orders before placement.
- Place/cancel/modify orders only after explicit exact user approval.
- Journal previews, executions, and reviews.
