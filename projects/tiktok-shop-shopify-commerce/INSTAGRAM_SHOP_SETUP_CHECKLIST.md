## Readiness checklist

### 1. Shopify store readiness

- [ ] Shopify online store exists.
- [ ] Store is not password-protected before commerce review / public shop activation.
- [ ] Sender email is valid.
- [ ] Store domain is ready for product checkout.
- [ ] Tax settings are reviewed in Shopify Settings → Taxes and Duties.
- [ ] Shipping, returns, privacy policy, and terms pages are ready.
- [ ] Product pages have clear titles, descriptions, prices, images, and availability.
- [ ] Products comply with Meta Commerce Policies and ad standards if ads will be used.

**Manual / approval-sensitive:** Shopify billing, domain, payment, tax, and public store settings require Affan approval.

### 2. Meta account readiness

- [ ] Facebook account exists for the operator.
- [ ] Facebook Page exists for the business/brand.
- [ ] Business portfolio / Business Manager exists and owns or manages the Page.
- [ ] Operator has full control/admin permission for the Page and business portfolio.
- [ ] Instagram account is converted to a professional account.
- [ ] Instagram professional account is associated with the business Facebook Page.
- [ ] Business is located in a country supported by Shops on Facebook and Instagram.
- [ ] Account/Page demonstrate trustworthiness and accurate business information.

**Manual / approval-sensitive:** Facebook/Instagram login, business portfolio changes, permissions, and account linking require Affan action.

### 3. Shopify sales channel setup

- [ ] In Shopify admin, go to Settings → Sales channels.
- [ ] Add **Facebook and Instagram by Meta** sales channel.
- [ ] Authorize the correct Facebook account.
- [ ] Connect the correct business portfolio.
- [ ] Connect the correct Facebook Page.
- [ ] Connect the correct Instagram professional account.
- [ ] Confirm catalog creation/sync.
- [ ] Confirm eligible products are available to the Facebook and Instagram channel.

**Important constraint:** Shopify/Meta use one catalog for Facebook Shop, Instagram Shopping, and Meta marketing. Making a product available to the channel can expose it across all configured Meta commerce surfaces.

**Manual / approval-sensitive:** Installing/authorizing the sales channel and publishing products require Affan approval.

### 4. Product/catalog readiness

- [ ] Choose 3-5 low-risk starter products.
- [ ] Avoid election/political, restricted, misleading, or claim-heavy products for first review.
- [ ] Use clean product images with no policy-risk text.
- [ ] Add product categories / Google Product Categories where needed.
- [ ] Confirm product inventory or fulfillment path.
- [ ] Confirm each product can be purchased directly from the verified Shopify domain.
- [ ] Confirm variants/prices sync correctly.

**Manual / approval-sensitive:** Public product publishing requires approval.

### 5. Instagram profile readiness

- [ ] Bio clearly explains the brand.
- [ ] Profile image/logo is set.
- [ ] Contact info is accurate.
- [ ] Website link points to Shopify/storefront or approved landing page.
- [ ] First 6-9 posts/Reels are brand-safe and not empty/spammy.
- [ ] Account status/recommendation eligibility is checked if available.

**Manual / approval-sensitive:** Public profile edits and posts require approval.

### 6. Review and launch

- [ ] Submit shop/catalog for Meta review if prompted.
- [ ] Resolve rejected products if any.
- [ ] Test product tagging after approval.
- [ ] Test user flow: Instagram product tag → product details → Shopify checkout.
- [ ] Confirm tax/shipping display and checkout behavior.
- [ ] Create first product-tagging post only after approval.

**Manual / approval-sensitive:** Review submission, product tagging, and first public post require approval.
