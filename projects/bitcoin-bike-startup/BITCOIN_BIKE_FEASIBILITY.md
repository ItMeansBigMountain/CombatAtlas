# Bitcoin Bike Startup - Feasibility Brief

## Goal

Assess technical and business feasibility of a Bitcoin-powered bike mining venture.

## Technical Assessment

- **Energy Requirements:** Approx. 1 kW per bike for sustained mining.
- **Hardware:** Need efficient ASIC miners that can operate on bike-mounted power.
- **Battery Capacity:** At least 2 kWh to sustain 8 h operation per day.
- **Cooling:** Airflow design needed to keep miners within safe temperature.
- **Integration:** Motor‑bike alternator can provide ~200 W continuous; supplemental solar panels could augment.

## Business Assessment

- **Capital Expenditure:** $2 k per bike (frame, battery, miner, mounting).
- **Revenue Estimate:** Assuming 0.001 BTC/day per bike (@ $30k/BTC) → $30/day → $900/month.
- **Break‑even:** ~7 months assuming 30 % overhead (maintenance, insurance).
- **Regulatory:** Verify local vehicle modifications, electrical safety, and cryptocurrency regulations.
- **Market:** Target urban commuters in high‑electric‑cost regions.

## Risks

- **Hardware Obsolescence:** Mining hardware degrades quickly.
- **Electricity Cost:** Battery wear and charging may offset gains.
- **Legal:** Vehicle modification restrictions.
- **Volatility:** BTC price swings affect ROI.

## Next Steps

1. Source a low‑power ASIC miner (~300 W).
2. Prototype mounting on a bike frame.
3. Run a 48 h pilot to collect hash‑rate & energy data.
4. Evaluate ROI with real‑world electricity costs.
5. Conduct legal review for vehicle modifications.
